## Scope limitation — read this before reusing anything

**These files contain aggregate statistics only. They contain no CHARLS
participant-level records, and nothing here permits access to CHARLS data.**

This license covers our own contribution — the derived aggregate estimates
and the rendered figures — and nothing else. It does **not** grant any right
in the underlying CHARLS data, which we are not able to license to you
because we do not hold those rights ourselves.

CHARLS data are distributed by the Institute of Social Science Survey,
Peking University, under a data-use agreement to which we are party as
registered users. That agreement **prohibits redistribution of the data**,
which is why no CHARLS data (raw waves, the harmonized extract, or the
derived participant-level file) appear in this repository.

If you wish to derive anything from CHARLS yourself — including re-running
these scripts — you must obtain the data in your own right:

1. Register at <http://charls.pku.edu.cn> and accept the data-use agreement.
2. Acknowledge CHARLS in any resulting publication as required by that
   agreement (see `NOTICE.md`).
